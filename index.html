    About & credits

    Dry fire only. Unload your firearm, empty your magazines and keep all ammunition out of the room.

    This app is an independent re-creation of Dry Fire Par Time Tracker by CSL1911A1 (apps4shooting.com), whose drill formats, drill sets and practice history it follows and whose backups it can import. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by CSL1911A1.

    Many of the starter drills and par-time goals are inspired by Ben Stoeger's training books, especially DryFire Reloaded and Dry-Fire Training: For the Practical Pistol Shooter, and Practical Shooting Training with Joel Park. The descriptions here are brief summaries written for this app. For the full drills, progressions and the reasoning behind them, get the books. This app is not affiliated with or endorsed by Ben Stoeger.
